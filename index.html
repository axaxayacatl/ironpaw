THE FIELD MANUAL

PRACTICAL REFERENCE / FIRST RESPONSE / RESPONSIBLE CARE

FIELD PROTOCOL IX-F001

WHEN YOU FIND A CAT

01 /ASSESS THE SITUATION

Observe the cat from a safe distance. Look for visible injuries, difficulty breathing, distress, or signs that kittens may be nearby. Avoid chasing or cornering the cat.

02 /CONTACT LOCAL SUPPORT

Contact a local animal shelter, rescue organization, veterinarian, or animal control service for guidance. Ask about safe handling, lost-pet procedures, and available assistance before attempting capture.

03 /CHECK FOR IDENTIFICATION

If the cat can be safely contained, ask a veterinarian or shelter to scan for a microchip. Check local lost-pet listings and follow applicable found-animal procedures.

04 /ARRANGE SAFE CARE

Follow a rescue professional's advice about temporary containment, food, water, and separation from household animals. Keep an unfamiliar cat in a quiet, secure area only if it is safe to do so.

DO NOT HANDLE AN INJURED OR FRIGHTENED CAT WITHOUT APPROPRIATE GUIDANCE. SEEK URGENT VETERINARY HELP FOR SERIOUS INJURIES OR BREATHING DIFFICULTIES.
FIELD NOTICE IX-F002

KITTENS WITHOUT A VISIBLE MOTHER

OBSERVE FIRST / INTERVENE WHEN NECESSARY
Young kittens may be left alone temporarily while their mother searches for food. Observe from a safe distance and contact a local kitten rescue or veterinarian for age-specific advice. Do not separate kittens from their mother unless intervention is necessary or a qualified responder advises it.

COMPANION RECORD IX-T001

THREE-INGREDIENT OFFERING

OAT FLOUR
PLAIN PUMPKIN
EGG
SIMPLE HOMEMADE TREAT REFERENCE

Combine plain pumpkin puree, oat flour, and egg, then bake until fully cooked. Do not use sweetened pumpkin pie filling. Allow to cool completely and offer only a small amount occasionally if the ingredients are suitable for your cat. Treats should supplement, not replace, a complete feline diet.
COMPANION RITUAL IX-C001

TWO-STEP CAT ACTIVITY

01 /THE HUNT

Hide a familiar toy or place a few pieces of your cat's usual food nearby. Let your cat investigate at their own pace, without forcing participation.

02 /THE RETURN

Sit quietly with your cat for a few minutes. Offer gentle contact if they welcome it, or simply share the space and let them choose how to interact.
