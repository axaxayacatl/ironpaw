    FELINE GROWTH / APPROXIMATE DEVELOPMENTAL MILESTONES

    REFERENCE TABLE IX-K002
    00–01 WEEK / NEONATAL Eyes are closed, ears are folded, and movement is primarily crawling and wriggling. Kittens depend entirely on their mother or an appropriate neonatal care plan.
    01–02 WEEKS / EARLY DEVELOPMENT Eyes begin opening and ears gradually unfold. Walking is not yet coordinated.
    02–03 WEEKS / SENSORY DEVELOPMENT Eyes are generally open. Ears become more upright. Kittens begin attempting to stand and walk.
    03–04 WEEKS / MOBILITY Walking becomes steadier. Baby teeth begin appearing. Curiosity and environmental exploration increase.
    04–06 WEEKS / SOCIAL DEVELOPMENT Play and exploration increase. Kittens may begin transitioning toward soft kitten food while continuing to nurse.
    06–08 WEEKS / WEANING Many kittens eat independently and have most or all of their baby teeth. Individual development varies.
    08+ WEEKS / JUVENILE DEVELOPMENT Kittens are typically mobile, playful, and eating independently. Continued physical and social development follows.

    FIELD NOTICE

    CARE NOTICE IX-K003

    AGE ESTIMATES ARE NOT A SUBSTITUTE FOR EXAMINATION

    Developmental characteristics overlap and may differ between kittens. Weight alone cannot establish age. A veterinarian or experienced kitten-care professional can assess age, hydration, health, and feeding requirements more reliably.

    URGENT WARNING: A kitten that is cold, weak, struggling to breathe, unresponsive, or unable to nurse needs prompt veterinary or rescue assistance. Do not delay care to complete this calculator.
